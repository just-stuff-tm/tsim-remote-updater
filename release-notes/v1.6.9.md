## Faster repeater settings
- **Up to 40 console commands per wake** (was 10), so **Read all** in the dashboard's Repeater settings finishes with one text instead of three.
- Safety limits for big batches: a batch stops starting new commands after 5 minutes, and after 3 commands in a row with no reply from the repeater (e.g. power saving) the rest are reported as skipped instead of each waiting 8 s.

## GPS
- Faster start: the T-SIM no longer waits up to 30 s for the modem's `READY!` message, which some modem firmware never sends. It waits at most 5 s, then accepts `AT+CGNSSPWR?` reporting on.

## Dashboard
- Detects T-SIM firmware 1.6.9 and sends Read all as one batch; older firmware still gets 10 per wake.

Install: press **Update T-SIM firmware** in the dashboard (about 3 minutes).
