## Location (GPS)
- **Get location** on the T-SIM tab reads the modem's built-in GNSS and shows the coordinates, fix time, satellites, accuracy, altitude and a small map. Coordinates stay blurred until you press the eye.
- Optional: auto-update the location on every check-in (off by default).
- **Set repeater location** on the LoRa radio tab sends the coordinates to the repeater (`set lat` / `set lon`) after you confirm.
- New system commands `GPSFIX` and `GPSCFG`, sent from the dashboard without an extra text.

Needs a GNSS antenna on the T-SIM's GPS/GNSS u.FL connector.
