## GPS fixes
- **GNSS antenna power**: switches on the modem's GPS antenna supply (`AT+CGDRT=4,1`, `AT+CGSETV=4,1`), as in LilyGO's GPS example for the T-SIM7672G. Without it an active antenna saw no satellites.
- Waits for `+CGNSSPWR: READY!` and checks `AT+CGNSSPWR?` before the search timer starts.
- Search timeout: default **180 s** (was 90 s), adjustable up to **600 s**. A first fix can take up to 5 minutes.
- GPS stays on for about 5 minutes after a try, so a retry soon after is a faster warm start. It switches off right away while low-battery protection has the repeater off.
- **GPS diagnostics** in the dashboard's Location card: READY wait, power check, antenna reply, GNSS mode, first and last raw modem reply, and satellites parsed.

## Dashboard
