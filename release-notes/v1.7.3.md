## Heltec reboot wake (optional wire)
Add one wire and a reboot of the Heltec repeater wakes the T-SIM. That includes a `reboot` sent over the mesh with stock MeshCore. The T-SIM then:
1. resets its modem (`AT+CFUN=0/1`, or a PWRKEY power-cycle if that fails). This re-initialises the SIM and network.
2. posts a heartbeat with the reason `heltec-reboot`.
3. fetches and runs queued dashboard commands, as on a watchdog wake.

So if texts stop reaching the T-SIM, you can still wake it from the mesh.

### Wiring (no resistor needed)
- Heltec **GPIO43 (TX)** → Schottky diode (1N5817 / 1N5819 / BAT54), **cathode toward the Heltec**, anode → T-SIM **GPIO6** (left header).
- **47–220 nF** ceramic from GPIO6 to GND (100 nF is ideal).
- Optional: 100 kΩ from GPIO6 to T-SIM 3V3. Not needed, because the T-SIM uses GPIO6's internal pull-up (~45 kΩ), awake and in deep sleep.

**Timing:** the diode pulls GPIO6 low within microseconds. The pull-up then recharges the capacitor: with ~45 kΩ and 100 nF (RC ≈ 4.5 ms), GPIO6 stays clearly low (below 0.25 × 3.3 V) for about 0.9 ms after each burst, and below the "high" threshold for about 6 ms. With 47 nF that's ~0.4 / 2.7 ms, and with 220 nF ~1.9 / 13 ms. The deep-sleep wake needs only microseconds, so any value works. The diode keeps GPIO6 from ever driving the Heltec's TX line.

### Safety
- GPIO6 wakes the T-SIM through ext1 (any low). The SMS ring on GPIO3 is unchanged (ext0). With the wake on, GPIO6 uses its internal pull-up (`INPUT_PULLUP` awake; RTC pull-up on, pull-down off, RTC peripheral domain kept powered in deep sleep). An unconnected GPIO6 reads high and never wakes the T-SIM.
- The wake isn't armed for a sleep unless GPIO6 is steadily high right before sleeping. That covers a missing wire, or the Heltec still printing.
- Accepted wakes: at least 60 s apart, at most 6 per 24 h. These limits are stored in NVS, so they survive restarts.
- A wake where GPIO6 stays low, comes too soon or goes over the limit counts as spurious. 3 in a row turn the feature off ("auto-off"). A Heltec reset the T-SIM did itself (REBOOT, after a Heltec update) is ignored and doesn't count.
- On/off: `WDCFG hwake=0|1`, on by default. Dashboard: T-SIM tab › Modem watchdog. Ticking the box again re-enables it after an auto-off.
- New counters in the heartbeat and on the dashboard: Heltec reboots detected, ignored, and "not armed".

### Bench test
1. Without the wire: the pull-up holds GPIO6 high, so there are no Heltec wakes (counters stay at 0).
2. With the wire: GPIO6 sits at about 3.3 V. Let the T-SIM sleep; the last log line says `Heltec wake armed`.
3. Press the Heltec RST button, or send `reboot` over the mesh. The T-SIM wakes and logs `Heltec reboot detected`. The dashboard shows "Heltec reboot detected → Watchdog: modem recovered".
4. Reboot the Heltec again within 60 s. The wake is ignored ("within 60 s").

## Unchanged
- Everything from 1.7.2: watchdog command fetch, always-armed timer, awake deadline, hardware watchdog and restart after repeated modem failures.
- No outgoing SMS. The OTA partition layout, TSIMUPDATE with rollback and NVS config are unchanged.
- WDCFG from older dashboards still works (no `hwake` = keep the current setting).

Install: press **Update T-SIM firmware** in the dashboard (about 3 minutes). The wire is optional; without it nothing changes.
