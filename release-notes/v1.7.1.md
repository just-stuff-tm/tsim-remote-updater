## Modem watchdog: self-recovery
Before this release, the T-SIM only woke up when a text rang its modem. If the modem dropped off LTE or hung while the T-SIM slept, texts couldn't reach it (Hologram holds them, with no "SMS delivered"), and the T-SIM stayed silent until someone power-cycled it on site.

1.7.1 adds a timer wake, every **3 hours** by default:
- **Check (a few seconds):** wakes the modem from sleep without resetting it, then checks `AT`, the SIM (`AT+CPIN?`), registration (`AT+CEREG?` / `AT+CREG?`) and data attach (`AT+CGATT?`).
- **Recovery (only if the check fails):**
  1. Soft: `AT+CFUN=0` then `AT+CFUN=1`.
  2. Hard: `AT+CPOF`, or a 3 s PWRKEY pulse if the modem doesn't answer, followed by the normal RESET + PWRKEY power-on.
  
  Every step has a timeout, and recovery stops after 5 minutes per wake. Afterwards text wake-up is set up again and the T-SIM goes back to sleep. After a failure it retries sooner (30 min, then 1 h, 2 h …). It skips recovery below 3.35 V.
- **Heartbeat:** after each check (or only after a recovery, if you prefer), the T-SIM posts a small heartbeat to the dashboard. It includes counters, the last 6 recovery attempts and how many texts are waiting on the modem. Attempts are also logged in NVS.
- **No surprises:** a watchdog wake never sends a text and never fetches queued console commands. Texts already stored on the modem only run on watchdog wakes if you turn that on (off by default).

## Dashboard
- **T-SIM › Modem watchdog:** status, last check-in, next check, counters, last recovery, recent attempts and texts waiting. Settings: interval (off, 30 min to 24 h), heartbeat on every check, and run stored texts. They're applied at the T-SIM's next wake, or right away with *Save & send now* (1 text).
- **Last seen and offline warning:** the header badge shows **Offline**, and a banner appears, when the T-SIM misses 2 expected check-ins. The T-SIM card shows how long ago it was last seen and how often it's expected to check in.

## Unchanged
- No outgoing SMS, the same OTA partition layout, TSIMUPDATE with rollback and the same NVS config.
- The new settings live in their own NVS namespace (`tsimwd`). Defaults apply right after the update: every 3 h, heartbeat on, stored texts off.

Install: press **Update T-SIM firmware** in the dashboard (about 3 minutes) once the T-SIM is reachable again.
