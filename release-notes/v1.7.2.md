## Never stays unreachable
1.7.1 added a modem watchdog. 1.7.2 closes the remaining ways the T-SIM could go silent until someone power-cycles it on site.

- **Watchdog wakes fetch dashboard commands.** After every successful watchdog check (default every 3 h) the T-SIM fetches and runs queued dashboard commands, so console commands, settings, **T-SIM updates** (TSIMUPDATE) and **Heltec updates** get through even if texts don't. One fetch per wake, no retries, and new commands stop starting after 2 minutes. Skipped below 3.35 V. Turn it off with `WDCFG cmd=0` (on by default).
- **Heltec update backup.** *Update radio firmware* still texts `UPDATE <url>`. For T-SIM 1.7.2+ the dashboard also queues a watchdog-only copy. The copy is used only by a watchdog wake and is cancelled as soon as the text starts the update. It expires after 24 h.
- **Always a timer wake.** Every sleep arms a timer (never longer than 24 h), even if no other timer is due. The watchdog can no longer be turned off: interval 0 now means every 24 h.
- **Hang protection.** Every wake has an awake deadline: 20 minutes, or 45 minutes while an update runs. When the deadline passes, the T-SIM restarts. The RTC hardware watchdog backs this up 60 s later, in case the CPU is stuck. Both are stopped right before deep sleep.
- **Restart after repeated modem failures.** After 3 failed recoveries in a row (and again after every further 3), the T-SIM restarts completely. The restart runs the full modem power-on, SIM and network setup again.
- **New counters** in the heartbeat and on the dashboard: restarts, hang resets and command fetches. They are stored in NVS `tsimwd` (`rst`, `dlx`, `cmdn`).

## Checked, no change
- Stock MeshCore on the Heltec doesn't print anything over serial on its own: only its boot line and CLI replies, with debug output off in release builds. The Heltec TX line goes to T-SIM GPIO41, which can't wake the ESP32-S3 from deep sleep anyway. So there's no serial wake from the Heltec.

## Unchanged
- No outgoing SMS. The OTA partition layout, TSIMUPDATE with rollback and the NVS config are all unchanged. `WDCFG` from older dashboards still works.

Install: press **Update T-SIM firmware** in the dashboard (about 3 minutes).
